Questions? Answered.

How long is the contract?

There's no long contract. All packages are month-to-month. I just ask for 30 days' notice if you'd like to stop.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, a few photos or short clips of your business (phone videos are fine) and 20 minutes a month to approve the plan. I'll guide you through the rest.

How quickly will I get my content?

Your first batch of content is ready within 7 working days of our first chat. After that, I deliver each month's content before the month starts. Premium clients get changes back within 24 hours.

Do I own the content?

Yes. Once you've paid for the month, all the ads and videos are yours to keep and use wherever you like, even if you stop working with me.

Is the ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Facebook/Instagram from your own account, so you stay in control. Most small businesses start with R300 to R1 000 a month, and I'll help you choose.
